SUMMERTIME FUN WITH MY BESTIES

Plan your perfect beach day with your girls too!:

Did you know? The average person only has about 80 summers in their entire lifetime! Why spend your days liking stories on social media when you could make those stories yourself? This summer, instead of doomscrolling our social media feed all day, my friends and I decided to take a step out into the bigger world and plan a trip to Cox's Bazar.

Here's what we did for our summer vacation:

It's a beach hangout combined with a road trip! We spent the time in the car singing our hearts to our favourite songs. Our laughs echoed in the car as we played a variety of games with each other. We took breaks amidst driving to buy snacks and chitchat.

Our journey came to a halt as we arrived at the Sea Pearl Beach Resort. We were welcomed with delicious drinks and luggage service. Quickly settling our stuff in our rooms, we hurried downstairs to explore the arcade area and visit their water park too.

Next up, we visited the Inani Beach in the evening. where we wrote our names in the sand and built small sandcastles together, only for them to be washed away by the waves.

Nearby, There were two opportunities waiting for us.

  • Surfing
  • Paragliding

We decided: Why not both?

When it came to surfing, only the bravest among our friends decided to challenge the harsh water. A test of steadiness, they fell again and again. Perhaps the surfing was never about mastering it as a skill but gaining a new experience.

Paragliding was a daunting matter especially as many of us were afraid of heights. But hearing from others who have done paragliding before, they made it sound as if it was a breeze. We decided to challenge our fear, setting up the straps and precautions, we flew up. In the end, even the most paranoid friend of ours instead admitted that she enjoyed relaxing high up in the sky viewing the scenary of the beach.

As the sun was setting at the horizon of the sea, the sky turned a pretty emulsion of pink and orange. We took our opportunity to snap some sun kissed pictures, each taking their turns to stand in front of the glittering blue waves crashing against the sand.
